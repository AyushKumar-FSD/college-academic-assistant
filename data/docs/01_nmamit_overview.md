# NMAM Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte - Overview
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/about-nmamit-nitte.php

Nitte Mahalinga Adyanthaya Memorial Institute of Technology (NMAMIT), Nitte was established in 1986 and is recognised by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi. Since June 2022 it has been a constituent college (off-campus centre) of Nitte (Deemed to be University), Mangaluru.

Rankings and recognition: NMAMIT is in the 151-200 rank band of the NIRF 2025 (Ministry of Education). It was placed in the 'Platinum' category for industry linkages in the AICTE-CII Survey of Industry-Linked Technical Institutes 2020. Nitte (Deemed to be University) is accredited with an 'A+' grade by NAAC and is placed under Category 'A' by the Ministry of Education (formerly MHRD).

Undergraduate programs: the institute offers B.Tech in fourteen disciplines - Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, Biotechnology, Civil Engineering, Computer & Communication Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Computer Science & Engineering (Cyber Security), Electrical & Electronics Engineering, Electronics & Communication Engineering, Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design & Technology), Electronics & Communication (Advanced Communication Technology), Information Science & Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Robotics & Artificial Intelligence.

NBA accreditation: seven UG programs are accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), New Delhi, under Tier-I - Biotechnology, Civil, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical & Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Information Science & Engineering and Mechanical. The MCA program is also NBA accredited.

Postgraduate programs: M.Tech (Computer Science & Engineering, Cyber Security, Mechatronics, Structural Engineering, VLSI Design & Embedded Systems), MCA, and MBA (through Justice K S Hegde Institute of Management). All departments have research guides for Ph.D. Basic science and humanities teaching is done by the Departments of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Humanities.

Campus and location: the campus is about 125 acres in the Western Ghats at Nitte, Karkala Taluk, Udupi District, Karnataka - 574110. The nearest airport is Mangaluru International Airport (45 km). The nearest railway stations are Udupi (40 km) and Mangaluru (50 km). Nitte is 19 km from NH-66 (Kochi-Panvel) and 7 km from NH-169 (Mangaluru-Solapur).
