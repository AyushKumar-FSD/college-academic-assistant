# Internship Guidelines for B.Tech Students (Nitte DU 2023 regulations)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/syllabus/ndu/2023-27/B.Tech._CS_Regulations_2023.pdf

## Overview
- Internships are mandatory for the B.Tech degree. A student who does not complete them is declared failed in the course and must complete them later. In total the two internships are 10 weeks and 10 credits (Internship-I 2 credits, Internship-II 8 credits).
- Each internship is done under a faculty mentor. Depending on the activity, it is done individually or in batches of not more than three students. Students keep a daily diary, which the mentor signs, and submit a report.

## Internship-I (activity based, 2 weeks)
- Duration: 2 weeks (80-90 hours) of inter- or intra-institutional activities, done during the first-year academic session or the vacation after the 2nd semester. Lateral entry students do it during the 3rd semester or the break between the 3rd and 4th semesters.
- Evaluation: 100 marks, CIE only, no SEE. Marks are 25 for the first presentation, 25 for the second presentation and 50 for the report and final presentation. A viva voce is held in the 2nd semester (3rd for lateral entry) and the 2 credits appear in the 4th semester grade card. Minimum for a pass is 40 marks.
- Example activities: soft skill training, contribution at the incubation or innovation cell, learning at departmental labs or the tinkering lab, consultancy or research projects inside the institute, coding, mini projects, debates and quizzes, technical paper surveys, hackathons, safety training, industrial visits with a report, and Indian Knowledge System topics.

## Internship-II (8 weeks in total)
- Done in the vacations between the 4th and 5th semesters and between the 6th and 7th semesters. A typical split is 2 to 4 weeks after the 4th semester and 4 to 6 weeks after the 6th semester, totalling 8 weeks. It can be an industry, government, NGO, MSME or rural internship, or an innovation or entrepreneurship activity, with a short societal internship of about 2 weeks and a 6-week industry internship, or a research / industry internship for the full 8 weeks (320-360 hours).
- It is evaluated in the 8th semester and carries 8 credits, with CIE 50 marks and SEE 50 marks (minimum 40 in the internship overall).

## Project work linked to internships
- Mini project in the 6th semester (about 6 hours per week, 2 credits). Major Project Phase I in the 7th semester (2 credits) and Phase II in the 8th semester (8 credits). Students may do the 8th semester project in a research institute, an industry or an intra-institute centre of excellence. The project report can be submitted only after completing 122 credits (88 for lateral entry), and the viva voce is individual.

## Activity points
- Every 4-year B.Tech student must earn 100 activity points (75 for lateral entry) as per the AICTE activity point program, spread over the program. Activity points do not affect SGPA or CGPA but the 8th semester grade card is issued only after they are earned.
