# Student FAQ - NMAMIT, Nitte (compiled from the college documents)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/rules.php

Q: What is the minimum attendance required?
A: 85% in each subject per semester. The Principal can condone up to 10% for valid reasons such as medical grounds, university-level sports or cultural activities, workshops or paper presentations. Below 75% a student cannot write the SEE of that course and gets an 'N' grade.

Q: What happens if my attendance is below 75%?
A: You are not allowed to appear for the Semester End Examination in that course, you receive an 'N' grade and you must re-register for the course when it is next offered in an odd, even or summer semester.

Q: How are marks split between internals and the semester end exam?
A: 50% Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and 50% Semester End Examination (SEE). You need at least 40% in CIE, at least 40% in SEE and 40% overall to pass.

Q: How many credits are required for the B.Tech degree?
A: 160 credits (120 for lateral entry students) and a CGPA of at least 5.00, plus 100 activity points (75 for lateral entry) and the mandatory internships.

Q: How many credits can I register for in a semester?
A: Between 16 and 28 credits in a regular semester, with an average of about 20. In the summer semester the limit is 16 credits.

Q: What is the grading scale?
A: O (90 and above, 10 points), A+ (80-89, 9), A (70-79, 8), B+ (60-69, 7), B (55-59, 6), C (50-54, 5), P (40-49, 4) and F (below 40, 0).

Q: What CGPA is needed for distinction?
A: A CGPA of 7.00 or more is First Class with Distinction, 6.00 to 6.99 is First Class and 5.00 to 5.99 is Second Class.

Q: What if I miss the semester end exam for a medical reason?
A: Submit the documents to the Controller of Examinations within 2 working days of the missed exam. If your attendance is at least 85% and CIE at least 70% you get grade 'I' and can write a make-up examination.

Q: Is re-evaluation allowed?
A: Yes, only for theory papers, by applying within the stipulated time and paying the prescribed fee. The marks after re-evaluation are final.

Q: How early must I reach the exam hall?
A: 20 minutes before the exam begins. Entry after the exam starts is not allowed.

Q: Are internships compulsory?
A: Yes. Internship-I is 2 weeks (2 credits) and Internship-II is 8 weeks in total (8 credits). Not completing them means you cannot be awarded the degree.

Q: What is the exam entry qualification and how do I get admission to B.Tech?
A: Through the NUCAT entrance test (General category) or direct NRI category admission. The admissions page asks for at least 50% in Class 12 PCM/relevant subjects. Applications are online at apply.nitte.edu.in.

Q: Is there a scholarship?
A: NUCAT toppers (20% of the seats in each program) get a scholarship of 20% to 40% of the prescribed fee. AICTE scholarship schemes are linked from the NMAMIT website.

Q: Which courses are in the 4th semester of B.Tech CSE?
A: Linear Algebra and its Applications, Design and Analysis of Algorithms, Microprocessor and Embedded Systems, Software Engineering and Project Management, Database Management Systems with its lab, Enhancing Self Competence, Python Programming with Data Science, and Essence of Indian Culture.

Q: Who handles placements?
A: Abhyuday, the Department of Counselling, Welfare, Training & Placement. Campus recruitment has been running since 1994, with more than 200 companies visiting.

Q: Can I do an Honours or Minor degree?
A: Yes, by earning 18 extra credits. Honours needs a CGPA of 7.5 and no backlog at the end of the 4th semester; Minor needs a CGPA of 5.0 and no backlog at the end of the 3rd semester.
