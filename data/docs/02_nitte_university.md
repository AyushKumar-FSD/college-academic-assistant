# Nitte (Deemed to be University) - Overview
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/

Nitte (Deemed to be University), commonly called Nitte DU, is headquartered at 6th Floor, University Enclave, Medical Sciences Complex, Deralakatte, Mangaluru - 575018, Karnataka. Phone +91 0824 2204300 / 01 / 02 / 03, email info@nitte.edu.in, admissions helpline +91 95131 88844.

The university website describes it as having more than 45 years of excellence, NAAC A+ accreditation, NIRF rank 80 (2025), a QS Asia ranking in the 600 band, and 160+ UG/PG programs in areas such as medicine, engineering and dentistry.

Constituent institutions include K S Hegde Medical Academy, A B Shetty Memorial Institute of Dental Sciences, Nitte Usha Institute of Nursing Sciences, Nitte Gulabi Shetty Memorial Institute of Pharmaceutical Sciences, Nitte Institute of Allied Health Sciences, Nitte Institute of Physiotherapy, Nitte Institute of Communication, Nitte Institute of Speech & Hearing, Nitte Institute of Architecture, Nitte University Centre for Science Education and Research, Nitte Institute of Hospitality Services, Nitte Institute of Professional Education, Justice K S Hegde Institute of Management (Nitte), NMAM Institute of Technology (Nitte), Dr N S A M First Grade College (Nitte), Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology (Bengaluru), Nitte School of Architecture, Planning and Design (Bengaluru), Dr N S A M First Grade College (Bengaluru), Nitte College of Pharmaceutical Sciences (Bengaluru) and Nitte School of Fashion Technology & Interior Design (Bengaluru).

Admissions for 2026-27 are applied for online at apply.nitte.edu.in. The website also lists sections for program fees, scholarships, examinations, IQAC/NAAC, NIRF, regulatory compliance, careers, the Student Grievance Redressal Committee, the Internal Complaints Committee, and policies and guidelines.

Recent news on the home page: in September 2026 Nitte University was re-designated, for a fresh five-year term, as an FAO Reference Centre for Antimicrobial Resistance.
