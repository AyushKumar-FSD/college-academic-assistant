# Placements and Career Support at NMAMIT (Abhyuday)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/placement.php

Placement and counselling are handled by Abhyuday, the Department of Counselling, Welfare, Training & Placement. The team consists of a department head, a full-time counsellor, an office superintendent, a placement executive, faculty coordinators, staff and student volunteers.

Campus recruitment began in 1994 when Kirloskar Snydergeneral was the first company to visit and hired two students. The site now reports more than 200 companies visiting the campus and more than 1200 job offers.

Services: orientation for fresh entrants, soft skills and leadership training, public speaking and communication training, mock interviews, aptitude, technical round, HR interview and group discussion preparation under 'Crack the Campus', talks by industry experts and alumni, Employability Readiness Programme, internship facilitation, preparation for higher studies and the armed forces, pooled campus drives for neighbouring colleges, peer learning for academically weak students and remedial programs for non-placed and non-IT students. Students who are not placed get help with off-campus placements and referrals for one year after graduation.

Japan connection: NMAMIT has MoUs with the University of Miyazaki and Ritsumeikan University since 2013 for student and faculty exchange and internships, and takes part in Sakura Science programs. Japanese industry placements started in 2019, with 35 placements in Japanese companies reported so far. Japanese language classes have been offered since 2019. Companies named include Nidec Read Corporation, Yokogawa Electric, Sasken Technologies, Kobayashi Create, Forent, Mamiya IT Solutions, Japan Research Institute, Miraic, ANDPAD, PLAN-B, Bravesoft, GA Technologies, SANTEC and AIBOD.

Highest packages shown on the placement page (batch year not stated on the page): Amazon 45.64 lakhs per annum for four students (CSE and ISE), Twilio 43 lakhs for two CSE students, Commvault 29.25 lakhs, Amazon 30 lakhs for three CSE students, Cohesity 22.42 and 24.5 lakhs, Nidec Read Corporation (Japan) 22.67 lakhs and 18 lakhs, Yokogawa Electric (Japan) 20.20 and 19 lakhs, Nutanix 18 lakhs, Kobayashi Create 17.5 lakhs and VMware 11.75 lakhs. Microsoft is also listed for a CSE student.
