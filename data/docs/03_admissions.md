# Admissions at NMAMIT (2026-27)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/admissions.php

B.Tech eligibility (as stated on the NMAMIT admissions page): pass in Class 12 or equivalent with at least 50% marks in aggregate in the optional subjects Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry / Biotechnology / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics, with English as one of the languages of study. The candidate must be 17 years old by 31 December of the year of admission. Note: the B.Tech Regulations 2023 document lists a lower minimum of 45% (40% for reserved category) - check the current admissions notice for the figure that applies.

Admission categories: General category and NRI category. General category means Indian nationals who studied Class 12 in India; admission is based on merit in the All India entrance test NUCAT (Nitte University Common Admission Test). Registration for NUCAT is online at apply.nitte.edu.in.

NUCAT scholarships: 20% of the seats in each program are set aside for NUCAT toppers, who receive a scholarship of 20% to 40% of the prescribed fee.

NRI category: direct admission on the basis of marks in the optional subjects of the qualifying exam, offered by inter-se merit. Eligible are foreign nationals, PIOs, Overseas Citizens of India, candidates who passed Class 12 outside India, and candidates who passed Class 12 in India but are sponsored by parents or blood relatives with NRI status. They submit the Admission Enquiry form on apply.nitte.edu.in.

Documents required for B.Tech admission: 10th certificate as proof of date of birth, 12th marks card, Transfer Certificate, Conduct Certificate, Migration Certificate (originals plus 3 attested copies for most), Eligibility Certificate issued by Nitte University (handled by Nitte), physical fitness certificate from a registered medical practitioner, blood group certificate, Aadhaar card copy, and photographs (5 passport size 35mm x 45mm and 5 stamp size 20mm x 25mm). NRIs additionally need the sponsor's passport and visa, residence proof, employment certificate, sponsorship certificate, a relationship certificate, and fee payment in foreign currency or INR from an NRE account.

Commencement: B.Tech classes start on the date prescribed by the university in July/August. PG classes start in August/September.

Lateral entry: a separate lateral entry fee structure for B.Tech is published; diploma holders join the second year (third semester).

B.Sc (Honors), 4 years: eligibility is Class 12 with Physics, Mathematics and Chemistry along with Biotechnology / Biology / Electronics / Computer Science and English. Admission is through KEA, and unfilled seats revert to the college. Total fee is Rs 60,000 (Rs 8,220 paid at KEA and Rs 51,780 at the college; direct admission students pay Rs 60,000 to the college).

M.Tech (2 years): bachelor's degree in the relevant field with at least 50% marks (45% for reserved category) and a valid NUCAT score. Specializations listed: Computer Science & Engineering, Structural Engineering, VLSI & Embedded Systems Design, Cyber Security, Mechatronics.

MCA (2 years, intake 180): bachelor's degree with Mathematics at 10+2 or degree level, at least 50% marks (45% for reserved category), and NUCAT.

MBA: graduate in any discipline with at least 50% marks; CAT / MAT / KMAT / XAT / ATMA scores are considered.

Fee documents published on the admissions page: B.Tech fee structure, lateral entry fee, hostel fee, bus fee (2025-26) and the PG fee structure. A brochure (NMAMIT Brochure 2025-26) can be downloaded from the site.
