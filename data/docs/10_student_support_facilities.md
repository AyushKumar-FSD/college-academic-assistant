# Student Facilities and Support at NMAMIT and Nitte DU
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/admissions.php

The NMAMIT website lists these campus facilities: hostels, sports, guest house, transportation (bus), library, student co-operative society, seminar hall and auditorium, canteen, health centre, and a bank and post office on campus. Separate PDFs are published for hostel fee and bus fee. The knowledge base does not contain timings, room rules or fee amounts for these facilities.

Student support listed on the site: mentoring and counselling, anti-ragging measures, a gender sensitization cell, a grievance redressal committee, a committee for SC/ST students, a document on differently-abled students, and the UGC handbook on cyber hygiene. Abhyuday provides a full-time counsellor.

Student info and portals: academic calendar page, results for the VTU batch, parent login (Nitte DU student portal), rules and regulations, and online certificate verification.

Associations: technical and non-technical clubs, NSS, NCC and Red Cross. Incridea is the technical and cultural fest hosted at the campus according to alumni testimonials.

University-level services of Nitte DU: hostels, conveyance, health care, laundry service, child care, a women-friendly campus, library resources, gymnasium, sports facilities, student clubs, NCC, mentoring and counselling, prevention of ragging, prevention of harassment, substance abuse prevention, an international students cell, a student wellness centre, and a Student Grievance Redressal Cell. The university also has an Internal Complaints Committee and an NUeLearn learning platform.

Ragging is banned and treated as a criminal offence under the regulations. Students can raise complaints through the grievance links on the Nitte website.
