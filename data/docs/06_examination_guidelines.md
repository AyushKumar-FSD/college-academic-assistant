# Examination and Evaluation Guidelines (B.Tech, Nitte DU 2023 regulations, Appendix B)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/nmamit/syllabus/ndu/2023-27/B.Tech._CS_Regulations_2023.pdf

## Theory courses (PCC / PEC / OEC)
- Scheme: CIE 50 marks + SEE 50 marks = 100 marks.
- CIE has two Mid-Semester Examinations and tasks. Mid Sem Exam-1 covers Unit-1 (about 40% of the syllabus, 15 teaching hours) and Mid Sem Exam-2 covers Unit-2 (another 40%, 15 teaching hours). Each mid-sem paper has two descriptive parts of 10 marks each (two questions set, one to be answered), so each mid-sem is 20 marks and the two together are 40 marks.
- The remaining 10 marks of CIE come from tasks: 5 class tests, quizzes or assignments conducted for each unit, all compulsory.
- To be eligible for the SEE a student needs at least 40% of the CIE marks (20 of 50) and the required attendance.

## IPCC and PBL courses (theory integrated with practical)
- CIE is 50 marks: theory 30 marks (60% weightage, from mid-sems and tasks) and practical / project based learning 20 marks (40% weightage). Minimum eligibility is 12 of 30 in theory and 8 of 20 in practical, 20 of 50 in total.
- SEE is 50 marks with a minimum of 20 marks to pass.

## Semester End Examination (SEE) pattern for theory
- The SEE is a 3-hour paper for 100 marks that is later reduced to 50 marks (fractions are rounded up to the next higher value) and added to CIE.
- Paper structure: MCQs on the entire syllabus carry 20 marks (10 questions of 2 marks or 20 questions of 1 mark, all compulsory). Unit-1 descriptive: 3 questions set, 2 to answer, 16 marks each (32 marks). Unit-2 descriptive: the same (32 marks). Unit-3 descriptive: 2 questions set, 1 to answer, 16 marks (16 marks).

## Laboratory courses
- CIE 50 marks: 30 marks for lab conduction and record (average over all experiments, each evaluated for 30 marks) and 20 marks for one internal lab test. SEE is 50 marks. At least 20 marks are needed in each of CIE and SEE.
- For software experiments the 30 marks per experiment are split as 12 for observation / program writing and execution, 6 for viva voce and 12 for record writing. The 20-mark lab test is split as 4 for write-up, 10 for execution and 6 for viva voce.

## Other components
- Mini project: 100 marks CIE only, minimum 40. Seminar: 100 marks, minimum 40.
- Internship-I (2 weeks) is 100 marks CIE only, minimum 40.
- Internship-II (8 weeks in total) and research internship / advanced industry internship / project work: 100 marks, minimum 40 in each of CIE and SEE.
- Mandatory non-credit courses (such as Environmental Studies, Constitution of India, Kannada) are assessed through CIE with a minimum of 40% and receive grade PP (pass) or NP (not pass). They carry no grade points but must be passed to receive the degree.

## Exam day rules
- Students must be in the examination hall 20 minutes before the exam begins. Entry after the exam begins is not permitted under any circumstances.
- Cases of unfair means or malpractice are reported to the Controller of Examinations for action.
- Make-up examinations are held during the summer semester as notified in the academic calendar for students with grade 'I' or 'X'.
