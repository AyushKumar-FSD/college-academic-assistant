# B.Tech Annual Fee Structure 2026-27 (NUCAT 2026)
Source: https://nitte.edu.in/fee/BTECH.pdf

The fee PDF lists four amounts (in INR) for every B.Tech program. The last amount, Rs 10,000, is the exam fee, which is collected at the start of each academic year together with the annual tuition fee. The three other amounts appear to be the tuition for the General category, the Scholarship category (NUCAT toppers) and the NRI category, but the column alignment cannot be confirmed from the extracted text, so students should confirm the exact column in the original PDF.

Hostel fee and bus fee are published as separate PDFs on the NMAMIT admissions page. Their amounts are not part of this knowledge base.
